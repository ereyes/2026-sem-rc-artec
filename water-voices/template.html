Water as source of performative text

Four families of movement. Words in five languages, shared vocal experiments, and the places or objects that make the sounds. Some words exist in different families.

All twenty sound spellings have voice takes: eight recorded examples and twelve AI-generated takes in one stock voice. Each circle adds an independent, repeating voice layer; use its level slider to shape the mix. Language buttons choose the visible words and the spoken languages.

The voices

Recorded and generated voices articulate the sound words. The twelve generated takes use Kokoro-82M’s af_heart stock voice and are marked as generated in the voice studio. All twenty sounds are ready to play; recording a replacement is optional. Clips have been trimmed, faded, converted to mono, and adjusted in level; playback speed varies slightly. Prepared recordings retain their listed source licenses.
