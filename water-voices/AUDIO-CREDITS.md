## Generated voice takes

The twelve previously unrecorded sounds were generated locally with [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), using the af_heart stock voice through [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx). These are AI-generated speech, not recordings of a human performer. The model is Apache-2.0 licensed; the ONNX wrapper is MIT licensed.

Inputs use explicit IPA phonemes to avoid reading letter names, with one voice across all twelve takes. The ssss and fshhhh clips retain only the consonants from generated carrier words (hiss and offshore); they are short spoken gestures. Two tik syllables are separated by half a second of silence. Edits include outer-silence trimming, short edge fades, level adjustment, and leading/trailing pauses. The manifest records exact inputs, retained regions, and file checksums.
